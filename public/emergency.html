What We Handle

Urgent Repair Categories

Fast, reliable stabilization for the unforeseen issues that put your home, safety, or security at risk. If you're unsure whether it qualifies, call — we'll tell you straight.

Active Leaks & Minor Plumbing Emergencies

  • Under-sink shut-off valve failures
  • Dripping fixtures that won't stop
  • Supply line bursts and bursts at connections
  • Overflowing or continuously running toilets

Major replumbing is referred to our licensed plumbing partners.

Security & Access Issues

  • Broken door locks and latches
  • Compromised entry deadbolts
  • Damaged exterior doors
  • Broken window latches and sash failures

Restoring safe, lockable entry is the priority.

Weather & Structural Urgent Fixes

  • Loose or wind-damaged siding
  • Draft and water intrusion seals
  • Failing caulking around openings
  • Compromised flashing and weatherproofing

Stop the intrusion, then schedule the full repair.

Minor Electrical Hazards

  • Tripping or flickering fixtures
  • Burnt outlet replacements
  • Light switch failures
  • Loose or sparking receptacle covers

Breaker or panel rewiring is referred to licensed electricians.

When to Call 911 or Your Utility First

Some emergencies are beyond a handyman and need the fire department, 911, or your utility provider before anyone enters the home. Your safety comes first — call the pros below, then call us for the repair.

Call 911 immediately for:

  • · Suspected gas leak or rotten-egg odor
  • · Active structural collapse or roof failure
  • · Downed or arcing power lines
  • · Smell of smoke or active fire
  • · Any immediate life-safety threat

Call the utility for:

  • · Gas leaks — DTE Energy: 800-947-5000
  • · Electrical utility emergencies — DTE: 800-858-5544
  • · Gas or electric (Consumers Energy): 800-477-5050
  • · Outages on the utility side of the meter
  • · Downed service drop to your home

Once the utility or first responders have made the scene safe, we're here for the repairs — broken doors, sealed leaks, replaced fixtures, and everything in between.
