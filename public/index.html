Pricing 4
How do your standard hourly rates work?
We use a Fair Upfront Pricing Model with zero hidden fees. Your visit begins with a Minimum Service Call of $100 for Zone A (within 20 miles) or $145 for Zone B (20+ miles), covering travel, diagnosis, and the first hour of labor. Any continuous labor after the first hour is billed in transparent 15-minute increments at a flat rate of $70 per hour. Our labor rates cover installation and service only — hardware and materials are not included. They are billed separately at a standard 20% supply markup, or you can supply them yourself at zero markup!
Do you offer flat-rate pricing menus?
Yes! For common, straightforward repairs, we provide flat-rate menu items. This gives you absolute certainty over costs before we even begin. Flat-rate items include straightforward installations (like deadbolts, faucets, ceiling fans, or smart doorbells), drywall patches, TV mounting, and gutter cleanings. Every flat rate covers the installation or service labor only — hardware and materials are not included and are billed separately, unless you supply them yourself. Check our full Rates page for a detailed pricing menu.
How are materials and hardware billed?
All listed rates cover installation and service labor only. You are welcome to supply your own materials, hardware, paint, or fixtures with zero markup. If you prefer that we source and pick up parts for your project, we provide them at our standard 20% supply fee.
Are there any hidden trip or diagnostic fees?
No. Your Minimum Service Call ($100 for Zone A / $145 for Zone B) covers the drive, diagnostic assessment, and up to the first full hour of labor on site. Any continuous labor beyond the first hour is billed transparently at $70/hour in 15-minute increments.
Services 6
What is AAA Handyman Services LLC?
AAA Handyman Services LLC is a professional local handyman company specializing in reliable home repairs, maintenance, punch lists, and minor updates. We serve homeowners across Oakland County, Michigan, and guarantee high-quality and transparently priced craftsmanship.
What are your specialized audits?
We offer three high-value specialized audits designed to inspect, diagnose, and secure your home:
1. Home Safety Audit ($100): Fall prevention, detector checks, trip hazards, and entry security.
2. Energy Tune-Up Audit ($100): Detect and seal minor on-the-spot drafts or air leaks.
3. Storm-Prep Check ($100): Exterior caulk seals, drainage, and gutter inspection.
Note: Audit fees are credited toward same-day repairs over $250.
Do you offer service packages and add-ons?
We do! Our 4-hour and 6-hour Handyman Time Packages help homeowners knock out a punch list in one scheduled visit. We also offer service add-ons such as dryer vent cleaning, smart lock setup, and smoke detector battery replacement that can be added to a standard service call.
What is your 1-Year Workmanship Guarantee?
We stand firmly behind the quality of our craftsmanship. If any installation, repair, or assembly fails due to workmanship within 12 months of service, we will return and make it right at no labor cost to you.
Can I combine multiple small repairs into one visit?
Yes. You can bundle your punch-list items under our standard hourly rate, or save with our 4-Hour ($279) and 6-Hour ($405) Handyman Time Packages to knock out multiple tasks in a single scheduled visit.
Do you handle after-hours or emergency repairs?
Yes. We offer priority dispatch 7 days a week for urgent home repairs—such as active plumbing leaks, compromised security locks, or sudden storm damage—billed at a flat $155 for the first hour and $100 per hour after.
