1 Our Services

AAA Handyman Services LLC provides high-quality residential handyman services and commercial handyman services, including home and facility repairs, maintenance, punch lists, and minor updates. Our core services include:

  • Carpentry & Trim: Interior/exterior woodwork & trim
  • Doors & Windows: Installation & repair of doors and windows

  • Drywall: Installation, repairs, patching & cracks
  • Minor Electrical: Outlets, switches, lights & fans

  • Home Security: Locks, deadbolts, cameras & safety alarms
  • Senior & Aging-in-Place: Grab bars & accessibility modifications
  • Commercial Services: Light interior & exterior upkeep, facility punch lists, and tenant turnover repairs for businesses

This list is representative, not exhaustive, and reflects the residential and commercial repairs, facility punch lists, maintenance, and minor updates we perform. Please see Section 2 for the scope and licensing terms that apply to all services.

2 Licensing & Scope of Work

AAA Handyman Services LLC operates in compliance with Michigan’s minor project exemption (MCL 339.2403). Work requiring a licensed residential builder or skilled trade is performed or subcontracted by licensed professionals.

Our Scope of Work

We take on residential and commercial repairs, facility punch lists, maintenance, and minor updates — from a single small fix to a multi-day punch list. Every job is quoted transparently, including labor, materials, and all fees, and each project is scheduled and staffed to match the scope of the work.
  • Residential home repairs, commercial facility repairs, maintenance, punch lists, and minor updates are quoted as a single, clearly defined scope of work, so you always know what is included before we begin.
  • Plumbing, electrical, and mechanical work require the appropriate skilled-trade license under Michigan law regardless of price. Any work in these areas that requires a licensed trade is performed or subcontracted by an appropriately licensed contractor.
  • Where a project requires a permit or licensed trade, we will let you know before work begins so it can be arranged properly.

4 Unforeseen Conditions & Change Orders

Unforeseen Conditions Clause

Any additional work, labor, or materials required due to concealed or hidden conditions—such as code violations, structural rot, mold, or damaged wiring discovered only after opening walls, fixtures, or components—are outside the initial scope of work. Such items will be documented, communicated, and approved by the client via a written change order before any additional work proceeds or related costs are incurred.

Every quote, flat-rate price, and package price we give is based on the conditions that are visible at the time of the estimate. In practice, this clause means:

  • We stop and tell you first. If we uncover a hidden condition, work in the affected area pauses until you have seen the issue and decided how you want to proceed.
  • We document it. The condition is described and, wherever practical, photographed so you can see exactly what we found.
  • Nothing is billed without your written approval. Added labor, materials, and revised timing are set out in a written change order. No additional work begins and no additional cost is incurred until you approve it in writing (including by text or email).
  • Some discoveries fall outside our scope. If a hidden condition calls for a licensed trade, a permit, or work beyond the minor project limits described in Section 2, we will say so and refer you to an appropriately licensed professional rather than take it on.
  • Declining is always an option. If you choose not to approve the additional work, we complete whatever portion of the original scope can be finished safely and correctly, and bill only for that.

Pre-existing and concealed conditions are not defects in our workmanship and are not covered by the limited warranty described in Section 6.

8 Safety & Hazardous Materials

Safety & Hazardous Materials Clause

The safety of our team, our customers, and your home comes first. If work encounters, or is likely to disturb, lead-based paint, asbestos, mold, or other hazardous materials, we will stop work in the affected area and follow the protocols below before continuing.
  • Homes built before 1978 (lead paint). For homes constructed prior to 1978, federal law (the EPA Renovation, Repair & Painting Rule) presumes the possible presence of lead-based paint. Any disturbance of painted surfaces will be handled using lead-safe work practices, and we will provide the EPA “Renovate Right” pamphlet before work begins where required.
  • Asbestos & other hazardous materials. If materials suspected to contain asbestos (such as older floor tile, popcorn ceilings, siding, or pipe insulation), mold, vermiculite, or other regulated hazardous substances are encountered, work in the affected area will stop immediately.
  • Work stoppage & certified abatement. AAA Handyman Services LLC does not perform hazardous-material abatement. If hazardous substances are confirmed or reasonably suspected, work will not resume in the affected area until the material has been tested and, where necessary, removed by a certified abatement contractor. We will document the condition, notify you, and refer you to an appropriately licensed abatement professional. Costs of testing, abatement, and any delays are the responsibility of the customer and are outside the original scope of work.
  • Pets must be secured. The customer is responsible for securing all pets away from the work area for the full duration of the service visit. This protects your pets from tools, dust, and materials, and protects our team from distractions or injury. If a pet cannot be safely separated from the work zone, please let us know in advance so we can reschedule.
