How the AI Estimate Works

1. Upload a Photo

Snap or upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP of the damage — a hole in the wall, a leaky pipe, a sticky door.

2. AI Analyzes It

Our estimator identifies the issue, gauges dimensions, lists materials, and estimates labor time against our published rates.

3. Submit for Confirmation

Review your preliminary range, then click Submit Quote. A real dispatcher confirms the final price and books the visit.

Estimates are AI-generated and preliminary. Final pricing depends on an in-person inspection, materials, and your service zone. Materials are billed separately — supply your own at no markup, or let us source them with a standard 20% supply markup.
