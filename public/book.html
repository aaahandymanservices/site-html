Quick Answers

What if I need to reschedule?

Call or text (248) 385-3432, or reply to the email we send you, and we'll move your visit to another window. There is no rescheduling fee. Whatever notice you can give helps — it lets us offer the window to another neighbor.

How are hardware and materials handled?

If AAA Handyman is handling, purchasing, or supplying materials for your project, a material deposit is required prior to starting the work. For labor-only jobs where the customer provides all materials, no upfront deposit is required. Materials provided by AAA Handyman carry a standard 10–15% supply markup, or you can supply them yourself at no markup. Full details are on the rates page.

When do I pay?

If AAA Handyman is handling, purchasing, or supplying materials for your project, a material deposit is required prior to starting the work. For labor-only jobs where the customer provides all materials, no upfront deposit is required. Remaining labor balances are paid once the work is finished and you're happy with it, backed by our 1-year workmanship guarantee.
