What the Seasonal Home Checklist covers

On each scheduled visit, your craftsman works through a whole-home safety and upkeep sweep so small issues get caught before they become expensive repairs.

  • Gutter & downspout clearing and flow check
  • Weatherstripping & door-sweep inspection
  • Smoke & CO detector test and battery swap
  • Caulking check at tubs, sinks & windows
  • HVAC filter replacement & vent check
  • Deck, railing & exterior stability review
  • Faucet, toilet & under-sink leak inspection
  • Written summary of anything that needs attention

How member pricing works on future service calls

  • Members book at a discounted hourly labor rate on any additional service call or project.
  • Priority scheduling — members go to the front of the calendar, including seasonal rushes.
  • The standard trip charge is waived within Zone A on covered visits.
  • Materials stay at our everyday pricing — supply your own at no markup, or we source them at our standard 20% supply fee.

Commitment & cancellation

  • Month-to-month — no long-term contract and no enrollment fee.
  • Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice; you keep any scheduled visit already booked.
  • No cancellation penalty — you simply stop billing at the end of the cycle.

Final terms are confirmed in writing when you enroll. Have a question first? Just ask — we're happy to walk through the details.
