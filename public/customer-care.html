The 1-Year Workmanship Guarantee

For 12 months from the day a job is finished, we stand behind the integrity of our labor as it was originally performed. If a repair fails because of how we did it, we return and correct it at no cost to you. That is a defined commitment, not an open-ended maintenance plan — and knowing where the line sits is what makes it worth something.

Read the full guarantee Submit a guarantee claim

What the guarantee covers

  • Labor performed by our craftsman, judged against the condition it was left in.
  • Workmanship faults that surface within 12 months of completion — fasteners backing out, a joint separating, an install that was not set true.
  • A scheduled return visit to put qualified work right — no diagnostic fee, no trip charge, no deductible.

What falls outside it

  • Normal wear and tear. Hinges, hardware, caulk, and finishes all loosen and age with daily use.
  • Material degradation. Product or manufacturer defects, and materials you supplied, carry their own warranties rather than ours.
  • Environmental and structural shifts. House settling, seasonal humidity and temperature swings, moisture intrusion, pests, storm or impact damage.
  • Later alterations by others. Work adjusted, modified, or repaired by another party after we left, plus misuse and deferred maintenance.

None of this means we turn away the work. Concerns outside the guarantee are still welcome — they are simply scheduled and quoted as a standard service call at our published rates. Coverage runs on all labor performed by our craftsman; see the full terms in plain language.

Our Review Process

A Structured Three-Step Review

Every request moves through the same three steps, in the same order, whatever the concern is. You should never have to guess where yours stands or what it might cost.

  1. 1

    Submit Details

    Complete the intake form below with the service date, the address, a clear written description, and a photo of the area in question. Specifics matter here: a dated photo and an accurate account of what changed are what allow us to evaluate the work properly the first time.

    Photo & written description

  2. 2

    Professional Review

    Requests are reviewed during regular business hours, in the order received. We pull the original job file, compare it against what you have sent, and determine the scope — whether the concern involves our workmanship or a separate cause. You get a written answer from the person who did the work, not a form letter.

    During business hours

  3. 3

    Assessment & Action

    Qualified workmanship issues are scheduled and corrected at no cost to you. If the review shows the cause sits outside our workmanship — or the request is for unrelated work — the visit is booked as a standard service call at our published diagnostic and trip rates. Either way, you know which it is, in writing, before anyone is dispatched.

    Scheduled callback visit

On out-of-scope visits: our standard service call is $100 within 20 miles, or $145 beyond it, and covers travel, on-site diagnosis, and the first hour of labor — then $70 per additional hour in quarter-hour increments. No fee is ever charged for a visit that turns out to be covered workmanship.
