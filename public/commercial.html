Commercial Handyman Services FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer commercial handyman services in Oakland County, MI?

Yes. AAA Handyman Services LLC provides commercial handyman services for offices, retail stores, restaurants, clinics, schools, and small businesses throughout Oakland County, Michigan, from Waterford and Troy to Royal Oak, Novi, and the surrounding communities.

How much do commercial handyman services cost?

Pricing starts with a minimum service call: $100 in Zone A (within about 20 miles of our Waterford base) or $145 in Zone B (extended county, 20+ miles). That covers travel, diagnostics, and up to the first hour of labor. We also offer a 4-hour facility package at $279 (Zone A) and a 6-hour facility package at $405 (Zone A). Continuous labor after the first hour is billed at a flat $70 per hour in quarter-hour increments, and materials are billed separately.

Do you work with property managers and small businesses?

Yes. We provide dependable interior and exterior handyman services for offices, storefronts, and small commercial properties, including turnover punch lists between tenant occupancies and common area maintenance, and can work around your operating hours.

Do you offer emergency service for urgent commercial issues?

Yes. After-hours emergency service is available 7 days a week for urgent repairs that affect safety or security, such as leaks or door and window problems ($155 first hour / $100 per hour after). Call (248) 385-3432 for priority response.
