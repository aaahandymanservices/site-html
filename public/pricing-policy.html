Pricing Policies

Clear pricing, before the work starts.

Two policies shape every invoice we send: how parts and materials are handled, and what happens the moment we find something nobody could see when the job was quoted. Both are written out here in plain language, so nothing on your final bill is a surprise.

Parts & Materials No Surprise Invoices
Parts & Materials

Parts & Materials Options

Every standard service price, hourly rate, flat-rate menu item, and package we publish covers installation and service labor only. Hardware and materials are never folded into those numbers — they are handled separately, so you can always see exactly what you are paying for labor and exactly what you are paying for parts. That leaves you two straightforward options on any job.

Option A

You Supply the Materials

Have the parts on site and ready to go, and we simply install them. There is no markup at all — you pay for labor and nothing else. Please confirm the exact make, model, and specifications before your appointment so nothing stalls the job mid-visit.

$0 materials markup

Option B

We Source the Materials

Skip the store run entirely. We shop, select the correct part, transport it, and warranty it. Materials are billed at cost plus a standard 20% supply markup, which covers our sourcing time, vehicle use, and the liability we take on for the parts themselves.

20% supply markup

Please note: materials we purchase under the 20% markup are covered by our workmanship warranty. Customer-supplied parts are not covered — we stand behind our installation either way, but we cannot warranty a part we did not select or source.

No Surprise Invoices Guarantee

How we handle unexpected issues or hidden damage.

Your quote reflects the conditions we can see when we price the job. Open up a wall, a fixture, or a component and a house sometimes has other ideas — concealed rot, mold, damaged wiring, or old work that never met code. When that happens, the process below runs every single time, without exception.

  1. 1

    1. We Stop

    Active work in the affected area pauses immediately the moment concealed damage, rot, mold, or a code violation turns up. Nothing further gets done while you are still in the dark about it.

  2. 2

    2. We Show You

    The condition is documented and photographed. We walk you through what we found, what it means for the job and the timeline, the options for repairing it, and what each option would cost.

  3. 3

    3. You Decide

    No additional work proceeds and no additional charge is incurred without your explicit approval through a written change order. Say no and we finish the original scope and bill only for that.

Concealed conditions such as code violations, structural rot, mold, or damaged wiring fall outside the initial scope of work. The full wording is in Section 4 of our Terms of Service.
