Maintenance & Cleaning FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer Maintenance & Cleaning in Oakland County, MI?

Yes. AAA Handyman Services LLC is based in Waterford and provides maintenance & cleaning services for homeowners throughout Oakland County, Michigan, from Waterford and Clarkston to Troy, Royal Oak, Novi, and the surrounding communities.

How much does maintenance & cleaning cost?

Pricing starts with a minimum service call: $100 in Zone A (within about 20 miles of our Waterford base) or $145 in Zone B (extended county, 20+ miles). That covers travel, diagnostics, and up to the first hour of labor. Continuous labor after the first hour is billed at a flat $70 per hour in quarter-hour increments, and materials are billed separately.

Do you offer seasonal maintenance in spring and fall?

Yes. Spring visits focus on gutter cleaning, deck repair, and window inspections; fall visits focus on weatherproofing, door sealing, and winterization so your home is ready for the season ahead.

Do you offer emergency service for urgent issues?

Yes. After-hours emergency service is available 7 days a week for urgent repairs that affect home safety or security, such as leaks or door and window problems ($155 first hour / $100 per hour after). Call (248) 385-3432 for priority response.

Ready for maintenance & cleaning?

Call for availability and same-week scheduling, or request a free quote online. No job too small.
