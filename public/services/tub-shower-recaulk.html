Tub & Shower Re-Caulk FAQ

Common Questions

Do you offer Tub & Shower Re-Caulk in Oakland County, MI?

Yes. AAA Handyman Services LLC is based in Waterford and provides tub & shower re-caulk services for homeowners throughout Oakland County, Michigan, from Waterford and Clarkston to Troy, Royal Oak, Novi, and the surrounding communities.

How much does tub & shower re-caulk cost?

Pricing starts with a minimum service call: $100 in Zone A (within about 20 miles of our Waterford base) or $145 in Zone B (extended county, 20+ miles). That covers travel, diagnostics, and up to the first hour of labor. Continuous labor after the first hour is billed at a flat $70 per hour in quarter-hour increments, and materials are billed separately.

Why does caulk keep failing in my shower?

Cracked or peeling caulk usually means the old bead was applied over moisture or has simply aged out. We fully remove the failing caulk, clean and dry the joint, and reseal it so the new bead lasts.
